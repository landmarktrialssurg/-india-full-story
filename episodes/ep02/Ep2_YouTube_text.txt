Who were the very first humans to walk in India, and how did they get here? From Homo erectus leaving Africa two million years ago, to stone tools at Attirampakkam dated to about 1.5 million years, the Narmada Man skull, the Toba super-eruption, and the arrival of modern humans, this is the story of India's Old Stone Age.
